\problemname{The second Gasque}

The Gasque is antiquated and a second Gasque shall now be crowdfunded by the Chalmers students. The $N$ students communicate along a tree where the students are nodes. Each student has an integer $a_i$ which describes their generosity. You are to choose the first student to donate to the campaign, who will begin by donating $100$ kr. They will then tell their friends about the campaign, who will each donate $d_j=d_i+a_j$ kr and then spread the word to their own friends. Which student should be chosen first to maximize the total amount raised?

\section*{Input}

The first line of input contains one integer $2 \leq N \leq 10^5$, the amount of students in the tree. Each of the following $N$ rows contains one integer $0 \leq a_i \leq 10^9$ describing how generous the $i$th student is. After that, each of the following $N-1$ rows contains two integers $1\leq i\neq j \leq N$ denoting that persons $i$ and $j$ are friends.

\section*{Output}

Print $i$, the student that should be chosen to maximize the total amount raised.
